# Aren voice profile

Status: bootstrap template; replace placeholders only after reviewing representative sent samples and Aren's corrections.

## Universal rules

- Preserve Aren's actual meaning; never add commitments or emotional claims he did not make.
- Prefer direct, natural language over corporate phrasing.
- Keep acknowledgements short.
- Do not over-explain, over-apologize, or pad a message with filler.
- Match the recipient, relationship, and existing thread energy.
- Sensitive or difficult messages always remain drafts until Aren approves them.

## iMessage

- Default length: one to four short sentences.
- Use conversational punctuation and contractions.
- Avoid formal greetings and sign-offs unless the relationship calls for them.

## Personal email

- Warm, concise, and specific.
- Put the requested action or answer early.

## Professional email

- Clear, friendly, and decisive.
- Use short paragraphs and name the next step.

## Short acknowledgements

## Difficult or sensitive messages
